**Proactive AI monitors your business tools and surfaces what needs attention before you have to go looking for it.** Instead of opening five dashboards and running three reports to find problems, you get a push notification that says: this invoice is overdue, this contract expires next week, this client hasn't responded in two weeks.

Below is a practical guide organized by business function. Each section covers: the common problem, how proactive AI addresses it, and what Salt specifically does for that function.

## Financial Monitoring

**The problem:** You send an invoice, it lands in the client's inbox, and then it disappears into the void. You don't notice it's unpaid until you're reconciling at month-end — or worse, when the client asks you to send it again. Late payments create cash flow gaps that compound. Studies show small businesses wait an average of 27 days past net-30 terms to receive payment.

**How proactive AI helps:** The system connects to your accounting or invoicing platform (QuickBooks, FreshBooks, Xero, etc.) and monitors invoice status in real time. When an invoice passes its due date without payment, the AI surfaces an alert. When a client has viewed the invoice but not paid, the AI flags that too. Some systems can auto-send a reminder sequence based on aging thresholds.

**What Salt does:** Salt connects to your accounting tool and tracks every invoice. When an invoice goes unpaid past its due date, Salt pushes an alert with the client name, invoice amount, days overdue, and a direct link to resend or follow up. If Salt detects a pattern — a client who paid net-30 for six months suddenly 10 days late — it flags that as a risk signal. Salt does not send the reminder for you (you choose how and when to follow up), but it stops you from discovering the problem weeks late.

## Client Communication

**The problem:** Clients go quiet. A proposal sits sent-but-unread for 10 days. A key stakeholder stops responding to email threads. You don't notice the silence until a week has passed and the project timeline is at risk. By then, you're chasing instead of leading.

**How proactive AI helps:** The AI monitors email and CRM activity. It tracks response times, email open rates, and the age of the last communication on each relationship. When a client's response time drops below their historical baseline, or when an important email goes unread for a set period, the AI surfaces an alert.

**What Salt does:** Salt monitors your email and CRM for communication gaps. If a client hasn't responded in 7 days (configurable), Salt flags it. If a deal in your pipeline has been sitting at the same stage for 30 days without movement, Salt surfaces it. Each alert includes the last message exchanged, the client's recent activity (or lack of it), and a suggested next step. Salt does not draft reply emails for you — it tells you who needs a call and why.

## Schedule Management

**The problem:** Double-bookings happen. Appointments get missed. You agree to a call at a time that conflicts with an existing commitment because you were glancing at your calendar on mobile. Each scheduling error costs you time, credibility, and in some professions, money.

**How proactive AI helps:** The AI monitors your calendar for upcoming conflicts, gaps that need filling, and appointments that lack preparation time. It can also detect if you've scheduled back-to-back client calls without a buffer — a common cause of burnout. Some systems can suggest optimal meeting times based on your work patterns.

**What Salt does:** Salt connects to your calendar and surfaces scheduling issues proactively. When a new event creates a conflict, Salt alerts you before the conflict becomes a problem. When a recurring client call hasn't been scheduled for the current week, Salt reminds you. When your calendar shows five consecutive days of internal work but a client milestone is approaching, Salt flags the mismatch. Salt does not auto-schedule — you remain in control of your calendar — but you never discover a conflict the minute the call starts.

## Contract Tracking

**The problem:** Contracts auto-renew. Retainers expire unnoticed. Licenses lapse. You find out about a contract expiration when the client mentions they've signed with someone else or when you realize you've been working outside the terms for three weeks. Missing contract milestones exposes you to liability, lost revenue, and awkward conversations.

**How proactive AI helps:** The AI monitors your contract management tool or scans email for contract documents. It tracks key dates: start date, end date, auto-renewal window, notice period, and milestone dates. When a date approaches, the AI sends an alert with enough lead time to act.

**What Salt does:** Salt tracks contract dates from connected tools and surfaces alerts at configurable intervals. A contract expiring in 30 days? Salt flags it with the client name, terms summary, and renewal instructions. A retainer that auto-renews next week if not canceled? Salt tells you. A milestone payment due upon completion of a phase? Salt reminds you. Salt does not negotiate contracts — it ensures you never miss a date that matters.

## Project Oversight

**The problem:** You're juggling multiple client projects. Each has its own timeline, budget, deliverables, and stakeholders. Without constant manual checking, one project drifts off course — scope creep goes unnoticed, budgets run over, deadlines slip. By the time you catch it, the damage is done.

**How proactive AI helps:** The AI connects to your project management tool (Asana, Monday, ClickUp, Notion, etc.) and monitors project health. It tracks: tasks approaching due dates, budgets nearing thresholds, projects without recent activity, and milestones at risk of missing their target. The AI surfaces only the projects and tasks that need attention — not a full status report.

**What Salt does:** Salt reads your project management tool and surfaces alerts by exception. A project budget that's 80% consumed with only 50% of the work done? Salt flags it. A task assigned to you that's due in 48 hours with zero progress? Salt surfaces it. A project that hasn't been touched in 14 days? Salt reminds you. Salt does not re-prioritize your task list — it points you to the projects that need your attention right now.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can proactive AI replace my bookkeeper or project manager?**

No. Proactive AI doesn't perform the work — it monitors the work and tells you when something needs attention. You still need a bookkeeper to reconcile accounts and a project manager to coordinate deliverables. Proactive AI makes them more effective by surfacing issues early.

**Does proactive AI work with my existing tools?**

It depends on the tool. Salt connects directly to QuickBooks, FreshBooks, Xero, Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Calendly, Asana, Monday.com, ClickUp, Notion, HubSpot, and others. Check the tool's integration list before committing.

**Do I need to train the AI on my business?**

No. Purpose-built proactive AI tools like Salt connect to your accounts and start monitoring immediately. You configure alert thresholds and notification preferences — you don't train models or provide sample data.

**How many alerts will I get per day?**

It depends on your business activity. Most Salt users receive 3-8 alerts per day across all five functions. The goal is signal, not noise. You can adjust thresholds — if you don't want to hear about invoices until they're 7 days overdue instead of 1, set that preference.

**What happens if I ignore an alert?**

Alerts persist until the issue is resolved. Most systems escalate — a gentle reminder on day 1, a stronger alert on day 7, and an "overdue" flag if the issue passes a critical threshold. Salt surfaces ignored alerts in your feed and includes them in daily summaries.

**Can I set up proactive AI for just one function, like financial monitoring?**

Yes. You connect only the tools you want monitored. If you only need invoice tracking, connect your accounting platform and skip the rest. Salt respects your scope.
