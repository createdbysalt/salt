# Brand Terms & Naming

Quick-reference guide for Salt Core terminology. For full voice guidance, see `bible/salt_core_voice_bible.md`. For complete brand positioning, see `bible/salt_core_identity.md`.

## Brand Architecture

**Salt Core** has a dual-brand system:
- **Salt Core**: The product (studio operating system). Voice is confident, clear, systems-focused.
- **SALT Studio**: Gabriella's client-facing creative studio (CreatedBySalt.com). Voice is warm, thoughtful, mission-driven.
- **The Bridge**: The product connecting them. Professional, clear, white-labelable.

## Product Ecosystem

### Primary Products
- **Salt Core**: The complete studio operating system (always two words, both capitalized).
- **The Core**: Short form for the Next.js foundation/infrastructure layer.
- **The Intelligence Layer**: Multi-agent AI for strategy generation and market research.
- **The Bridge**: Client portal and command center (white-labelable, warm-professional voice).
- **The Velocity Layer**: Monthly updates and maintenance system.

### The 6-Layer Architecture
When referring to system layers, use:
1. **Infrastructure** (or "The Engine")
2. **Intelligence** (or "The Brain")
3. **Operations** (or "The Flow")
4. **Community** (or "The Network")
5. **Velocity** (or "The Maintenance")
6. **Finance** (or "The Fuel")

## Common UI Terms
- **Project**: A client engagement with structured deliverables and tracking.
- **Task**: A specific action item inside a project.
- **AI audit**: Automated analysis output tied to a project (lowercase "audit" in running text).
- **Studio**: Refers to a user's professional entity (not "agency" or "business").
- **Dashboard**: The main control center.
- **Workspace**: Where users manage their studio.

## Auth Terms
- Use **"sign in"** / **"sign out"** (two words).
- Avoid "log in/log out" unless already established in the UI.
- **Magic link** (lowercase "link"): Passwordless sign-in via email.
- **Account**: Not "profile" in user-facing UI.

## Email Conventions
- **From (magic links)**: `Salt Core <noreply@saltcore.io>`
- **From (transactional)**: `Salt Core <hello@saltcore.io>`
- **Support contact**: `hello@saltcore.io`

## Capitalization
- **Button labels**: Sentence case ("Create project", "Save changes").
- **Headings**: Sentence case unless the design system dictates otherwise.
- **Product names**: Always capitalize correctly ("Salt Core", not "salt core" or "SaltCore"; "SALT Studio", not "Salt Studio").
