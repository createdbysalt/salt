## OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPTS

### "Why should I pay monthly instead of buying a boilerplate once?"

**Answer:**
"Great question. You actually DO own the infrastructure code forever—just like a one-time boilerplate. The subscription covers three ongoing services:

1. **The Bridge** - Hosted client portal (ongoing server costs)
2. **Studio Intelligence** - Real AI API costs for generating reports
3. **Monthly updates** - New features, security patches, components

Think of it like this: ShipFast charges $299 for infrastructure code. You're getting that PLUS strategic intelligence (Competely charges $29/mo for this) PLUS a client portal (SuperOkay charges $57+/mo) for $99/mo. After 3 months, you've paid the same as ShipFast, but you also have intelligence and delivery tools.

Plus, 0% platform fee—you keep 100% of client revenue."

"I get it. Let me share the math that might help:

Or look at it this way: One $12k project pays for an entire year of Studio Professional. If you're doing even 2-3 projects per year, the ROI is 10x+.

The real question: Can you afford NOT to have the tools that help you win and deliver $10k+ projects?

We also have a 7-day money-back guarantee. Try it on your next client pitch. If the strategic intelligence doesn't immediately change how clients perceive you, get a full refund."
