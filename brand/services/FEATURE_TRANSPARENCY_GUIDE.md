# FEATURE TRANSPARENCY GUIDE
## How to Communicate Unfinished Features & Build Trust with Early Adopters

---

## Core Principle: Radical Transparency

**Never hide what's missing. Early adopters LOVE being part of the journey if you're honest. They HATE feeling misled.**

Early adopters will forgive missing features if you're transparent. They'll never forgive feeling deceived.

---

## The Five Trust Principles

1. **Never lie** - Don't mark something as available if it's not
2. **Never hide** - Don't omit information hoping people won't notice
3. **Always timeline** - Give specific quarters, not vague "soon"
4. **Always value** - Explain why current state is still valuable
5. **Always honest** - "We're not Queue yet" is better than pretending

#### Q: Why are you launching before everything is built?

**Three reasons:**

1. **Studio Intelligence is production-ready** - This is our unique value and it works beautifully. SALT Studio uses it for real client projects.

2. **We want your input** - Rather than guess what operations features matter most, we want early adopters to help us prioritize the roadmap.

3. **You can use it today** - The core system (intelligence + basic delivery + infrastructure) is fully functional. You can win and deliver $10k+ projects right now. The additional features make it even better, but they're not blockers.

If you need everything perfect before you start, we're not ready yet. If you want the strategic intelligence advantage today while we polish operations features together, join us.

---

#### Q: What happens if a promised feature doesn't ship on time?

We're transparent about timelines, but software development is unpredictable. If a feature is delayed:

1. We'll communicate the new timeline as soon as we know
2. Early adopters won't be charged more when it does ship
3. We'll prioritize based on what early adopters actually need most

You're not buying vaporware—the core value (Studio Intelligence) is ready today. The additional features are improvements, not core functionality.

---

#### Q: How does your client portal compare to Queue or SuperOkay right now?

**Honest answer:**

Our operations are simpler than Queue (they've been building for 5+ years). We cover the professional basics: branded login, project overview, file sharing, messaging, invoicing.

But we're the ONLY platform giving you AI strategic intelligence integrated with client delivery. 

**The choice:**
- Queue: Excellent operations, zero strategic intelligence, you still compete on price
- Salt Core: Strategic intelligence that wins premium projects + professional basics that deliver them

Queue is great if you already know how to win $10k+ projects. Salt Core helps you WIN them first, then delivers them professionally.

## Red Flags to Avoid

### ❌ DON'T DO THIS:
- Marking future features as available
- Using vague "Coming soon" without timelines
- Hiding limitations in fine print
- Pretending to be more complete than you are
- Over-promising features you're not sure about
- Ignoring questions about current state

### ✅ DO THIS INSTEAD:
- Clear badges with timeline quarters
- Honest "Here's where we are" messaging
- Prominent FAQ about current state
- "Not for you if..." sections
- Under-promise, over-deliver
- Proactively address limitations

---

## The Trust Equation

**Trust = (Credibility × Reliability × Intimacy) ÷ Self-Orientation**

**How transparency builds each factor:**

**Credibility:** "We're honest about what's missing" → You believe what we say about what's working

**Reliability:** "We ship what we promise when we promise" → Consistent behavior builds trust

**Intimacy:** "We share our roadmap and reasoning" → You feel like an insider, not a customer

**Self-Orientation:** "We admit delays and why" → You know we care about your success, not just our revenue

---

## Key Takeaway

**Early adopters don't expect perfection. They expect honesty.**

If you're transparent about:
- What works today
- What's coming when
- Why it's valuable even incomplete
- What happens if things change

...they'll become your biggest advocates.

But if they feel misled, even once, you've lost them forever.

**Radical transparency isn't just ethical—it's your competitive advantage.**
