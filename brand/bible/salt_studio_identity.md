# SALT Studio Core Identity

*This file defines the foundational identity of SALT Studio — who we are, what we believe, and how we show up. This is shared across voice and visual applications.*
---

## Brand Essence

SALT is the essential element that brings out what's already good — subtle, transformative, and impossible to ignore once it's there.

---

## Mission

We draw out the inherent beauty and truth in mission-driven organizations, then craft digital experiences that let that essence shine.

---

## Brand Promise

We don't make you into something you're not. We refine what's real, elevate what matters, and create digital spaces where your best self can breathe.

---

## Core Values

| Value | What It Means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Thoughtful craft over quick fixes** | We take time to understand before we create |
| **Human connection above all** | Technology serves people, not the other way around |
| **Authenticity without apology** | We help you be more of who you already are |
| **Quiet confidence** | Our work speaks; we don't need to shout |

---

## Our Audiences

We serve three primary audiences, each requiring tone adjustments:

### Church
Faith-driven organizations seeking digital presence that honors their mission and community.

- More reverent, less playful
- Emphasize stewardship and service
- Honor tradition while embracing accessibility

### Studio
Creative agencies, design firms, and fellow makers who value craft.

- Peer-to-peer, collaborative
- Craft and process-focused
- Show, don't just tell

### Startup
Emerging companies with bold ideas, limited resources, and urgent timelines.

- Energetic and forward-looking
- Acknowledge constraints honestly
- Balance ambition with groundedness

---

## Brand Personality

**In Words (Voice):** Thoughtful, warm, and confident without being overly polished. We speak like experienced creative partners who genuinely care about our clients' mission.

**In Visuals:** Human. Effective. Clear. Warm but professional. We blend organic elements (salt, rock formations) with clean, modern digital design.

---

## Decision Framework

When creating anything for SALT, ask:

1. **Does this feel like SALT?** (Human, effective, clear, warm but professional)
2. **Would this work in our portfolio?** (Elevated, intentional, not messy)
3. **Can this be systematized if it works?** (Repeatability matters)

If yes to all three, proceed. If no to any, return to the guidelines.
